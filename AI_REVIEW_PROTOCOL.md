# AI-assisted review protocol and provenance

Date: 9 November 2026.

Source: the previously retrieved Team-Logicflow study_run3 frozen sample and corpus, with uploaded sampled review templates. This is an AI-assisted draft layer, not an assertion that the original human-coding plan has been fulfilled.

## Sample coding

Two separately initialized AI reviewers each read all190 complete question-answer records using the original codebook. They were instructed not to inspect each other's decisions or the earlier regex-score proposals. They assigned four specificity scores0–3 and three binary explicit same-control links, retained exact source quotations and explained uncertain, prospective and negative claims. They did not communicate labels before recording their decisions.

The reviewers are AI processes using the same underlying model family. Separate contexts provide procedural separation, not independence of training, errors or judgement in the statistical/human-validation sense. No human reviewer identity or work is asserted.

## AI reconciliation

The root AI compared the two recorded score vectors. For134 answers, all seven scores agreed, so consensus was retained; those consensus answers were not independently reread by the root as a third complete review. For56 answers with at least one disagreement, the root reread the full disputed source answer, compared both explanations and wrote a specific reconciliation reason. Decisions can differ from either review. Scores were not averaged. All original A/B ratings remain preserved. This is AI adjudication, not a team meeting between two people.

## Main report review

AI full-report coders partitioned the53 reports and read every available question-answer record for their assigned reports, including blank/administrative/noneligible context. Positive evidence citations use eligible source rows only. They identified and aggregated salient actual controls for the four stages and recorded role/context-based applicability. Selected units can span several answers; repeated stage controls were matched only when source meaning supported the match. All-zero placeholders were used only for a reviewed applicable stage without a current disclosed control. Missing answers alone were not treated as proof of inapplicability or absent internal governance.

This was one AI main-control coding pass per report, not two independently adjudicated full-control enumerations. The root subsequently performed structural/provenance checks; those checks do not independently verify every semantic main-control judgment. Selection is not exhaustive. Human review is still required to assess the selected control universe, harmonise aggregation and verify stage relationships, including report changes across time.

All main-control scores were conservatively capped2 because the coders did not inspect external artifacts. Sampled-answer coders sometimes used3 for identifiable references stated in source text; those ratings are explicitly provisional. Before using final depth/prevalence claims, human reviewers must harmonise this distinction and verify the traceability references.

## Programmatic checks

Verified sampled IDs and source text preservation, all score domains, exact supporting quotations, eligible report/answer IDs, unique unit/control-stage keys, link-quote containment and exactly212 report-stage records. Applicable-stage pairs correspond to coded units; inapplicable stages have no units. These tests establish consistency and textual provenance, not scientific validity, link semantics, artifact accessibility, true corporate governance operation or completeness of disclosed controls.

The existing validation/evaluation code ran with these inputs in a temporary copy where original frozen-byte hashes could be reproduced. Any metrics from that compatibility exercise compare dictionary predictions against AI-reconciled reference labels and AI-to-AI agreement. They are not human-validated accuracy or independent human reliability and are not supplied as final scientific findings.

## Required reporting

Disclose this AI-assisted procedure, model/context dependence, lack of external artifact verification, nonexhaustive control selection, and outstanding human verification. Do not represent the files as independently human-coded, externally audited, comprehensive control prevalence, internal effectiveness or journal acceptance. The original CODEBOOK.md is retained unchanged so deviations from its intended human-review process remain visible.
